How the score works: this is a heuristic built from the same signals detectors use, not ZeroGPT's actual model. It's a mix of burstiness (how much sentence length varies), AI tells (stock phrases, em dashes, formulaic transitions), sentence openers (how often sentences start the same way) and vocabulary repetition. Lower means more human. Always run the result through the real detector you care about and reread it for meaning. Phrases flagged as AI tells are highlighted in the original.
